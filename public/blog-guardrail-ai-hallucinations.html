How Guardrail AI Works

Guardrail AI sits between your LLM and your end user. Every AI response passes through our scoring engine, which analyzes it for:

🔍 Hallucination Detection

Identifies fabricated citations, invented statistics, unverifiable biographical claims, and suspiciously precise numbers — the exact patterns that make AI outputs dangerous.

⚖️ Domain-Aware Risk Scoring

Automatically detects high-stakes domains (medical, legal, financial, security) and applies stricter thresholds. An AI suggesting a medication dosage gets different scrutiny than one recommending a restaurant.

🧪 Claim Verification

Extracts factual claims from AI responses and verifies them against Wikipedia in real-time. Verified claims boost confidence; contradicted claims trigger escalation.

🎯 Query-Context Matching

Compares the AI's response against the original user question to detect scope creep, irrelevance, and dangerous queries answered without appropriate refusal.
